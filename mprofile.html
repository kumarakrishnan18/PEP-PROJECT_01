Mentor guidelines

Principles for fair and meaningful review.

01
Review objectively

Evaluate the quality of the answer, not the identity or popularity of the student.

02
Reward useful thinking

Points should reflect how effectively a response solves or improves the student's problem.

03
Encourage practical solutions

Clear and understandable approaches should be valued over unnecessary complexity.

04
Protect the community

Review reported content and take appropriate moderation action when community rules are violated.

05
Keep points meaningful

Students cannot award themselves unlimited points. Mentor approval remains the final authority.
